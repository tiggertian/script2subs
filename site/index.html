Verbatim bilingual subtitles,

You write the script, record the voice, and Script2Subs lines your text up against the audio with AutoSubs — then exports DaVinci Resolve-ready SRT in one language or two. Your words are never rewritten.

Download for macOS Apple Silicon · unsigned (see install)

Requires AutoSubs for transcription — or bring your own SRT / timings JSON.

Install in about a minute

  1. Download & drag

    Open the DMG and drag Script2Subs into Applications.

  2. Clear the unsigned-app flag

    The build isn't notarized yet, so first launch is blocked. Run this once in Terminal, then open the app:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Script2Subs.app"

    If you see “unidentified developer,” right-click the app → Open also works. A “damaged” message is the same quarantine issue — the command above fixes it.

  3. Make subtitles

    Pick your narration and your scripts in the dashboard, then export bilingual.srt straight into Resolve.

What it does

Verbatim

Your script text is never paraphrased or auto-corrected. Alignment only decides when each line appears.

Bilingual by design

Keep a primary and secondary script as a strict line-for-line pair and get one stacked track with both languages in sync.

CJK-aware

Character-based wrapping and punctuation-aware breaks for Chinese and other CJK scripts, plus reading-speed checks.

One cue per line

Re-flow at phrase boundaries instead of guessing breaks that split words in half.

Review built in

A local app plays the audio, highlights each cue, and flags lines that are too fast or too long.

Resolve-ready

Exports primary, secondary, and bilingual SRT plus a timing report, with timecode offsets for non-zero timelines.
